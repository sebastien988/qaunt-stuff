## How the prediction is made

1. Average the daily news tone over the last 30 days, using only news published before the day of the prediction.
2. Sort that value into low, middle or high, compared with all earlier days.
3. Look up what Nvidia's share price did over the following trading days after every past day in the same group.
4. Report the average move, the median, the range covering 8 of 10 cases, and how often the price rose.

## Limits

- The forecast range is wide; see the average miss above.
- The news signal measures wording, not facts. It does not know what the stories are about or whether they are true.
- The history starts in 2017, a period in which Nvidia's share price rose strongly, so the averages lean positive.
- Multi-day outcomes overlap from one day to the next, so the day counts overstate how much independent evidence there is.
- This file is rewritten each time the script runs. The numbers go out of date as prices and news change; check the generated date.
